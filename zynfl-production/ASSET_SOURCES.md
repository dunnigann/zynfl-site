# Asset sources

Map geometry: [Natural Earth US state polygons](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_1_states_provinces.geojson), projected to Albers equal-area coordinates. [Natural Earth public-domain terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). Alaska and Hawaii are excluded from the contiguous-US view.

Manager portrait thumbnails are derived from the existing user-provided manager photos. Originals are retained.

Sports logo images come from ESPN’s team-logo CDN. The Palantir logomark uses the exact SVG paths on the company homepage, recolored dark for the light badge background. The India flag is from FlagCDN. Logos and marks remain the property of their respective owners.
